AIRIV Indonesia Clinical Operations

Indonesia Clinic Management & SatuSehat FHIR RME Engine

Community-ready clinic and pharmacy workspace for Odoo 18 with patient registry, clinical encounters, SOAP records, ICD-10 diagnosis support, prescriptions, and SatuSehat FHIR bridge surfaces.

Odoo 18 CommunityClinic RMESatuSehat FHIRICD-10AIRIV

Core Capabilities

AIRIV Clinic Indonesia turns core Odoo records into a reviewable clinical operations workspace for patient intake, encounter documentation, pharmacy flow, and interoperability preparation.

Patient Registry

Identity and contact

Maintain patient medical record numbers, NIK, demographics, contact details, and responsible party context.

Clinical RME

Encounter and SOAP

Record clinical encounters, subjective and objective findings, assessment, plan, diagnosis, and clinical notes.

Interoperability

SatuSehat FHIR bridge

Configure organization and integration profiles for controlled SatuSehat FHIR payload preparation and audit review.

Feature & Workflow Automation

1

Configure clinic

Set clinic identity, service units, practitioners, and integration profile before opening operations.

2

Register patient

Create or find the patient record, verify NIK and contact information, and keep one medical record number per patient.

3

Document encounter

Open an encounter, capture SOAP notes, add ICD-10 diagnosis, and record prescriptions or follow-up instructions.

4

Review integration

Validate required identifiers and inspect the prepared FHIR exchange record before any external transmission.

Installation Guidance

Install

Add to Odoo addons

Clone branch 18.0, place the module on the addons path, restart Odoo, update the Apps list, and install.

Configure

Set clinic context

Configure clinic identity, practitioners, service units, pharmacy settings, and integration profiles.

Operate

Run clinical workflow

Register patients, document encounters, record diagnosis and prescriptions, then review integration readiness.

Configuration Checklist

Patient identityVerify NIK, medical record number, date of birth, and contact data before clinical use.
Clinical completenessReview SOAP sections, diagnosis, prescription, and follow-up before closing an encounter.
Access controlReview clinical user groups and restrict sensitive records to authorized personnel.
FHIR readinessValidate organization, practitioner, patient, and encounter identifiers before exchange.
